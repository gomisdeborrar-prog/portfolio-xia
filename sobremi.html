Hi I'm Alexia!

    Hi! I'm Alexia Kleeberg, also known as Alexiaklee. I'm a concept artist, illustrator, and comic artist passionate about creating compelling worlds and characters. The strongest aspect of my work is my use of light, color, and atmosphere to tell stories and evoke emotion. Through my illustrations and designs, I aim to create images that feel immersive, expressive, and memorable.
